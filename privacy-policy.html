Privacy policy

This policy is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, together with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it. Where we say "we", "us" or "Lime Digital", we mean Lime Digital Institute. Where we say "you", we mean any visitor, enquirer, student, parent, business owner or alumnus whose data we handle.

01Who we are

Lime Digital Institute is a digital marketing training institute based in Rajkot, Gujarat, India. We run classroom and online courses, including the Foundation Track and the Advanced Track, for students, working professionals and business owners.

For the purposes of the DPDP Act, Lime Digital Institute is the Data Fiduciary: we decide why and how your personal data is processed, and we are responsible for it.

Registered entity: [Legal entity name, e.g. Lime Institute Pvt. Ltd. / proprietorship name], 5th Floor, LIME Institute, Jyoti Nagar Main Rd, near Masoom School, behind Crystal Mall, Ghanshyam Nagar, Rajkot, Gujarat 360005.

02Scope of this policy

This policy covers personal data we collect through:

  • our website limedigitalinstitute.org and its sub-domains, landing pages and forms;
  • enquiries by phone, WhatsApp, email, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube;
  • ads we run on Google and Meta platforms, including lead forms;
  • admission, attendance, assessment, certification and placement-support activities;
  • events, free trial classes, webinars, seminars and campus visits.

It does not cover the separate practices of third-party sites or apps you reach from our pages. Their own policies apply.

04How we use your data

  • To answer your enquiry: call, message or email you about the course you asked about, batch timings and fees.
  • To run your course: admission, class schedules, study material, attendance, assessments and certificates.
  • To support your career or business: share internship and job openings, or business growth resources, if you opted in.
  • To process payments: fee receipts, invoices, GST records and refunds.
  • To improve our website and courses: understand which pages and topics help visitors, using aggregated analytics.
  • To send updates you agreed to: new batches, workshops, webinars and offers. You can opt out at any time.
  • To show relevant ads: reach people who visited our site with ads on Google and Meta, only with your cookie consent.
  • To meet legal duties: tax, accounting and audit records, and responses to lawful requests from authorities.
  • To keep things safe: prevent spam, fraud and misuse of our website and systems.

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

05Consent and legal basis

We process your personal data on one of two grounds allowed by the DPDP Act:

  • Your consent, which you give when you submit a form, tick a box or accept cookies. Consent is free, specific, informed and unambiguous, and limited to the purpose shown at the time.
  • Certain legitimate uses under Section 7 of the Act, for example when you voluntarily share your details for a stated purpose and have not said you object, or when we must keep records to comply with law.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent as easily as you gave it: reply STOP on WhatsApp, use the unsubscribe link in any email, block or delete cookies in your browser as described in the cookie policy, or write to us at the address in section 16. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, and we may still keep records the law requires.

07Calls, WhatsApp and email

By sharing your number on our forms, you allow our counselling team to call you and message you on WhatsApp about your enquiry, even if your number is registered on the National Do Not Call (DND) registry. This permission covers your enquiry only.

  • We send promotional messages only to people who agreed to receive them.
  • Reply STOP on WhatsApp, or use the unsubscribe link in emails, to stop promotional messages.
  • Calls with our counselling team may be recorded for quality and training. We will tell you at the start of the call.
  • Students are added to batch WhatsApp groups for class updates. Other members of a group can see your number; tell us if you would rather receive updates one-to-one.

08Photos, videos and reviews

We sometimes take photos or videos at classes, events and campus visits, and share student work, reviews and success stories on our website and social media.

  • We ask for your written consent before using your name, face, voice, work or review in marketing.
  • You can ask us to remove your content at any time. We will take it down from our own website and active accounts; we cannot recall copies that others have already shared.
  • Reviews you post on Google or other public platforms are governed by those platforms' terms.

10How we protect it

We use reasonable security safeguards, in line with the DPDP Rules and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules, 2011, including:

  • HTTPS encryption across the website and forms;
  • role-based access, so team members see only what their work needs;
  • two-factor authentication on our CRM, email and ad accounts;
  • regular backups, software updates and access reviews;
  • confidentiality commitments from staff and contracts with data processors.

If something goes wrong

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law, explain what happened, and tell you the steps you can take to protect yourself.

Your duties: please give us accurate information, and do not register on behalf of someone else without their permission.

12Children's data

Our courses are designed for adults. Under Indian law, anyone under 18 is a child. If someone under 18 wishes to enrol, we collect and process their data only with verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, who must complete the admission form with them.

  • We do not run behavioural tracking or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • We do not use a child's photos or reviews in marketing without a parent's written consent.
  • If we learn we collected a child's data without proper consent, we will delete it promptly.

13Transfers outside India

Some of our service providers, such as Google, Meta and Zoho, may store or process data on servers outside India. Such transfers follow the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Government of India, and we choose providers that offer strong security and contractual protections.

14Third-party links

Our website links to and embeds content from other services, such as YouTube videos, Google Maps and social media profiles. When you interact with them, those services may collect data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to read them.

15Changes to this policy

Cookie policy

This cookie policy explains how Lime Digital Institute uses cookies and similar technologies on limedigitalinstitute.org. It forms part of the privacy policy above.

C1What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your browser. They help the site remember your choices, keep forms working and understand how people use it. Similar technologies include pixels (tiny images or scripts that tell an ad platform a page was visited) and local or session storage.

  • First-party cookies are set by our own website.
  • Third-party cookies are set by services we use, such as Google and Meta.
  • Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser; persistent cookies stay until they expire or you delete them.
