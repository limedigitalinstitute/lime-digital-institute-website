Integrity & Standards

Editorial & Verification Policy

At Lime Digital Institute, educational excellence is anchored in transparent facts, verifiable placement metrics, and continuously audited curriculum standards.

1. Curriculum Review & Industry Alignment

Our 16-module digital marketing and AI syllabus is reviewed every quarter by a panel of active agency directors, performance marketers, and hiring partners. Obsolete tactics are removed immediately, and new developments (such as emerging generative AI models, algorithmic shifts, and privacy tracking regulations) are integrated in real-time.

2. Placement & Outcome Verification

All placement statistics published by Lime Digital Institute — including our 800+ placed graduates, 93% placement rate within 60 days, and student salary figures — are cross-verified with official offer letters, corporate appointment letters, and hiring partner confirmation records. We do not inflate salary averages or cite speculative projections.

3. Live Ad Budgets & Practical Simulation Guarantee

We believe digital marketing cannot be mastered through textbooks or theory alone. Every student is provided access to live campaign simulations and real ad spend on Meta Ads and Google Ads. Campaign outcomes, metrics (ROAS, CPA, CTR), and case studies represent genuine client accounts or structured classroom ad simulations.

4. Responsible AI & Ethical Tool Training

Students are trained on 60+ industry AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, n8n, Midjourney, Perplexity) with strict emphasis on ethical disclosure, data privacy, hallucination verification, and compliance with search engine guidelines against programmatic spam.

5. Corrections & Public Transparency

If you identify any discrepancy in course documentation, blog articles, or public placement reporting, our academic team promptly investigates and publishes corrections. Contact our editorial desk directly at digital@limeinstitute.org or visit our campus at 5th Floor, LIME Institute, Jyoti Nagar Main Rd, Rajkot.
