SEO + CRO

Antra Auto Tech: from invisible to a 10-week fix plan

Antra Auto Tech (Antra Industries), Metoda GIDC, Rajkot, makes and exports engine valves, valve guides, seat inserts and steering bearings for OEM and aftermarket buyers.

RDRipal D.Lime Digital Institute student
Search visibility audit · antraautotech.com 1 / 12 pages indexed by Google 308Pages indexed by top competitor 84%Images with no alt text 11Pages hidden from search
1 / 12Pages indexed by Google
308Pages indexed by the top competitor
84%Images with no alt text (115 of 137)
7Critical conversion issues of 25
4,400US monthly searches for "engine valves"

Measured during the audits in September 2026.

01 · The challenge

antraautotech.com wasn't showing up in search. A canonical tag on every page pointed Google back to the homepage, so only 1 of 12 pages was indexed. Placeholder text and no analytics made the site hard to trust and impossible to measure.

02 · What Ripal built
01

Technical SEO & search visibility audit (13 pages)

Indexing, sitemap, JavaScript-set titles, soft 404s, image alt text and a competitor benchmark, ending in a prioritised action plan.

02

CRO audit & implementation plan (31 pages)

25 issues, each with a marked-up screenshot, fix steps, owner and success metric, plus a 5-phase, 10-week roadmap and a GA4 measurement plan.

03

US keyword research workbook

71 keywords pulled, a 40-keyword purchase-intent shortlist, a page-1 competitor sheet and six new pages to build.

04

Dealer reward programme

QR plus unique-code scratch cards with server-side validation, planned as a 1,000-box pilot.

05

Ad creative and logo lockups

For product promotion.

Brand + Packaging

Pletinus Group: an agri-export brand that sells on trust, not price

Pletinus Group, Rajkot, exports spices, pulses, seasonings, noodles & pasta and dehydrated foods to importers, distributors and food manufacturers across the Middle East, Europe, Africa, the USA and Asia.

AMAbhishek M.Lime Digital Institute student
16Catalogue pages
5Product categories
7Pouch designs
9Business Model Canvas blocks
01 · The challenge

Overseas buyers had been burned by delayed shipments and weak paperwork from Indian traders, and Pletinus had already lost a deal on price. The brand needed to win on reliability and documentation instead of discounts.

02 · What Abhishek built
01

Marketing strategy

A 7Ps marketing mix and a full Business Model Canvas, including a QR traceability idea and farmer storytelling.

02

Brand guidelines

Positioning, archetype, buyer segments, messaging pillars, voice, logo, colour, type and channel playbooks, plus a 7-page brand book.

03

16-page export catalogue (Edition 2026)

Company story, quality and certifications, process, SKU grids per category, private label and a QR contact page.

04

Packaging range

Branded stand-up pouches for Peanuts, Coriander, Green Gram, Fenugreek, Fennel, Black Chickpeas and Cumin, plus jar, poly-pack and PP-bag mockups.

05

AI product visuals

Category heroes for spices, pulses and pasta, and a website hero banner.

Brand Identity

SureSpool: one promise, carried from the spool to Instagram

SureSpool makes 1.75 mm PLA 3D-printer filament in Rajkot, Gujarat.

JBJenish B.Lime Digital Institute student
11Page brand identity
6Logo colourways
2Concepts explored
01 · The challenge

Makers lose prints re-calibrating every time they switch to a new spool. The brand had to own one clear promise: one tested print profile for SureSpool PLA, with no re-calibrating when you switch spools.

02 · What Jenish built
01

Logo

A "filament S" icon where the strand leaving the spool becomes the letter, plus a custom wordmark and six colour variations.

02

Colour and type

Calibration Yellow and Ink Black with Inter, plus clear-space and misuse rules.

03

Packaging

Spool front, side label, kraft carton, shipping box and a QR print-profile card inside every box.

04

Digital

Instagram feed, website, favicon and social avatars.

05

Print

Business card, stickers, invoice and photo watermark.

06

Concept exploration

An earlier coral-and-cobalt direction tested before the final identity.

Shirayuki: a premium menswear label, from logo to shopping bag

Shirayuki - "white snow" in Japanese - makes suvin-cotton formal wear for premium-minded men aged 25-35, with custom embroidery and add-ons like collar pins, pocket squares and scarves.

NVNiyati V.Lime Digital Institute student
3Logo lockups
3Brand colours
4Packaging mockups

Give a new label an identity that feels elegant, premium and comfortable, and that holds together everywhere a customer meets it - from the Instagram grid to the shirt box.

02 · What Niyati built
01

Logo system

Horizontal, stacked and circular icon lockups pairing SHIRAYUKI with 白雪.

02

Colour system

Who we are, who we serve, what sets us apart and the brand promise.

Brand + Content

Dhritiva Ventures: a complete brand system from a single logo mark

Dhritiva Ventures is an export and import trading brand built around one line: "Bridging Borders, Building Trust."

DPDrashti P.Lime Digital Institute student
8Brand colours
8Guideline sections
5Semrush Academy certificates
01 · The challenge

Turn a single logo mark into a brand system complete enough to run a business on - from web components to the email signature.

02 · What Drashti built
01

Logo system

Circular stamp mark and serif wordmark, with clear space, minimum size, approved backgrounds and misuse rules.

02

Colour and type

An 8-colour palette led by Ink Navy and Trade Gold, with Playfair Display and Karla.

03

Design system

Imagery rules, spacing and radius tokens, and UI components: buttons, cards, inputs, badges and icons.

04

Voice and tone

"warm, plainspoken", with do's and don'ts, plus a branded email signature.

05

Social content

A "Rose for Lakshmi" fragrance-guide post for Dhritiva Incense, and brand videos.

06

Certifications

Five Semrush Academy certificates: SEO Crash Course, International SEO, Content Marketing, Social Media Marketing and Marketing Analytics.
