Stay safe: pay only to the bank account, UPI ID or payment link given by Lime Digital Institute, and always take a receipt. We never ask for your OTP, UPI PIN or card PIN. If someone asks for these in our name, do not share them and write to us at once.

We want every class to be a safe and useful place to learn. As a student you agree to:

  • not record, copy or share class sessions, study material or login details without our written permission;
  • take care of the campus, computers and lab equipment, and pay for damage you cause;
  • not use our name, logo or certificate in a misleading way.

If these rules are broken, we may give a warning, suspend your access or end your admission. If we end an admission for misconduct, no refund is due.

Refund policy

We would like you to join only when you are sure. This policy tells you exactly when you can cancel and how much comes back to you. It is part of the terms above.

R1Where refunds apply

  • 3-day free trial: it costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.
  • Paid programs: the Digital Marketing Course, Diploma Degree Program, Bachelor's in Digital Business and Master's in Digital Business follow the schedule below.
  • Masterclasses and workshops: if a paid event is cancelled by us, you get a full refund. If you cannot attend, you can ask to move to the next event.

R2Refund schedule

The refund depends on when we receive your written request (see R5), counted from the start date of your batch.

When you askWhat you get back
Before your batch starts100% of the fees paid
Within 3 days after your first class75% of the fees paid
From day 4 to day 7 after your first class50% of the fees paid
After 7 days from your first classNo refund. You may use the batch shift option in R3 instead.
For longer programs: for the 3-year Bachelor's and the 11-month Master's, the schedule above applies to the fee of the term or instalment you have paid at that point. Fees for later terms that are not yet paid are cancelled when you withdraw, and you owe nothing further for them.

If a payment gateway or bank keeps a processing charge that it does not return to us, that amount is deducted from the refund.

R3Batch shift option

If you cannot continue because of a genuine reason, such as illness, a family emergency or a job transfer, you can ask to move your admission to a later batch of the same program instead of taking a refund.

  • Ask within 14 days of your first class and share simple proof of the reason.
  • It is allowed once per student and is subject to seat availability.
  • There is no extra fee for the shift. The fee you paid is carried over to the new batch.

R4When no refund applies

  • Your request comes after the windows in R2 have closed.
  • Your admission was ended for misconduct or false information (Terms, sections 04 and 06).
  • You missed classes or stopped attending, or you changed your mind after the refund window.
  • You received a certificate and have completed the program.
  • You paid money directly to someone else, such as an ad budget to Google or Meta, a domain, hosting or a software subscription. These are not Lime Digital Institute's fees, so we cannot refund them.
  • The fee was a discounted or scholarship fee, and the offer said it is non-refundable.

If we cancel your batch or cannot run it, none of these exclusions apply. Section 10 of the Terms gives you a full refund or a seat in a later batch.

R5How to ask for a refund

Please send a written request. A call alone is not enough, but you are always welcome to call first and we will help you.

  • Email digital@limeinstitute.org with the subject "Refund request".
  • Include your name, phone number, program and batch, fee receipt number, and the reason.
  • We acknowledge your request within 48 hours and tell you our decision within 7 working days.

R6How you get your money

  • Approved refunds go back by the same method you paid with: the same bank account, UPI ID or card.
  • If you paid in cash, we transfer the refund to a bank account in the student's name.
  • The money is sent within 7 to 10 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • We give you a refund note or credit note for your records, with GST adjusted as the law requires.

R7Instalments and failed payments

  • Instalments: if you withdraw, write to us before the next instalment is due so that it can be cancelled.
  • Money debited but no receipt: send us the transaction ID or a screenshot. If the payment did not reach us, your bank or gateway normally reverses it in 5 to 7 working days, and we will help you follow it up.
  • Paid twice by mistake: we refund the extra amount in full.

R8Escalation and contact

If you are not happy with a decision, write to our Grievance Officer. We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and aim to resolve them within 30 days. You may also approach a consumer commission as described in section 13 of the Terms.
