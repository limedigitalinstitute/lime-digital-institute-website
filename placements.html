Our Process

How we get you placed

A tracked, four-step placement process - not a vague promise on a brochure.

Step 01

Resume & portfolio build. We rebuild your resume and put together a live campaign portfolio that shows employers real, executed work - not just a certificate.

Step 02

Mock interviews. Repeated one-on-one interview practice with real feedback, so the first tough question you face is in practice, not in the actual interview.

Step 03

Employer network intro. Direct introductions to our hiring partner network of agencies, D2C brands, export businesses and e-commerce startups actively looking for talent.

Step 04

Post-placement support. Our support doesn't end at the offer letter - we stay available for guidance through your first few months on the job.
