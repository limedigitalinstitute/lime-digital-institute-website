Comprehensive 3-Year Roadmap

A Structured, Progressive Curriculum

From core creative and digital skills to full-scale performance funnels, unit economics, and venture launch.

Semester 1: Digital Core & Visual Design

Month 1–6
  • Digital Marketing Ecosystem: Channels, consumer psychology, customer journey mapping.
  • Graphic Design & Ad Creatives: Figma & Canva Pro, visual branding, typography.
  • Website Architecture: Domain, hosting, WordPress & Elementor responsive UI design.
  • Copywriting & Content Strategy: Psychological hooks, ad copy formats, email copywriting.
Semester 1 Capstone

Design and launch a live personal brand website and publish 5 high-converting ad creative sets.

Semester 2: Organic Search & Generative AI

Month 7–12
  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO): On-page, technical audits, schema markup, Semrush.
  • Generative AI for Marketers: ChatGPT advanced prompting, Midjourney, Claude, ElevenLabs.
  • Organic Social Growth: Instagram algorithm mastery, Reels storytelling, LinkedIn branding.
  • Influencer & Affiliate Marketing: Outreach frameworks, commission structures, campaign tracking.
Semester 2 Capstone

Rank 3 target keywords on Google page 1 and build a 10,000+ reach social media community.

Semester 3: Performance Ads & Conversion Math

Month 13–18
  • Meta Ads Manager (Deep Dive): CBO/ABO setups, custom audiences, CAPI, lookalikes, creative testing.
  • Google Ads Ecosystem: Search, Performance Max, YouTube Ads, Display, remarketing funnels.
  • Analytics & Tracking: Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Tag Manager, custom event tracking.
  • Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO): A/B testing, heatmaps with Hotjar, landing page friction audits.
Semester 3 Capstone

Deploy real ad spend on Meta & Google Ads with verified ROAS > 3.0x for real partner brands.

Semester 4: E-Commerce, D2C & Automation

Month 19–24
  • Shopify Architecture: Store setup, liquid fundamentals, high-converting product pages.
  • Marketing Automation & CRM: Klaviyo email flows, WhatsApp automations via Wati, Make.com.
  • D2C Logistics & COD Management: Courier integrations, NDR handling, RTO reduction tactics.
  • Marketplaces Growth: Amazon Seller Central, Flipkart advertising, catalog optimization.
Semester 4 Capstone

Launch a complete live Shopify D2C brand and generate 100+ paid customers within 30 days.

Semester 5: Unit Economics & Agency Scaling

Month 25–30
  • Business Finance & Unit Economics: CAC, LTV, contribution margins, burn rate, cash flow models.
  • Digital Agency Operations: Client pitching, proposal decks, scope of work, contract negotiations.
  • Growth Hacking & Viral Loops: Product-led growth, referral mechanisms, retention engineering.
  • Brand Governance & PR: Crisis management, brand positioning, investor pitch storytelling.
Semester 5 Capstone

Acquire a real business client, pitch a full-funnel growth roadmap, and manage an active retainer.

Semester 6: The Launchpad & Placements

Month 31–36
  • The Launchpad Venture Pitch: Pitch your startup idea to Angel Investors on Demo Day.
  • Executive Portfolio Defense: Live jury defense of campaign results, dashboards, and P&L.
  • AI-Assisted Career War Room: LinkedIn profile optimization, live mock panels, technical assessments.
  • On-Campus Hiring Drives: Direct interviews with hiring partners, digital agencies, and D2C brands.
Semester 6 Capstone

Final placement into top growth roles or venture incorporation with seed mentor support.
