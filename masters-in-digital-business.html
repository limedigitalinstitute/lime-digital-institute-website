Academic Rigor

11-Month Term-by-Term Architecture

A high-octane 4-term curriculum moving from technical performance execution to strategic leadership and enterprise campaigns.

Term 1: Performance Ads & Analytics Core

Months 1–3
  • Meta Ads Engineering: Advanced CBO scaling, dynamic catalog ads, conversion API, attribution windows.
  • Google Ads Mastery: Search intent matching, Performance Max, YouTube video action campaigns.
  • Web Intelligence & GA4: Custom explorations, conversion funnels, Looker Studio automated client reporting.
  • Conversion Rate Optimization: Heatmap behavior teardowns, A/B landing page testing frameworks.
Term 1 Milestone

Deploy real ad spend across Meta & Google Ads with verified ROAS > 3.5x for partner businesses.

Term 2: Inbound Growth, MarTech & AI Automation

Months 4–6
  • Technical & AI SEO: Core Web Vitals, entity-based search optimization, generative search engineering.
  • E-Commerce & D2C Architecture: High-conversion Shopify setup, apps ecosystem, marketplace optimization.
  • Marketing Automation Workflows: Klaviyo retention flows, WhatsApp CRM with Wati, Make.com triggers.
  • Generative AI for Marketing Leaders: Custom GPTs, automated content workflows, Midjourney ad pipelines.
Term 2 Milestone

Build an automated lead retention and sales engine generating recurring organic revenue.

Term 3: Business Strategy & Executive Capstone

Months 7–9
  • P&L & Unit Economics: Blended ROAS, CAC:LTV ratios, contribution margin modeling, burn management.
  • Digital Agency Operations: Client pitching, commercial contracts, team management, billing retainers.
  • Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Launching new products, category creation, enterprise sales frameworks.
  • Enterprise Live Capstone: Manage a live ₹1,50,000 marketing campaign for a corporate client.
Term 3 Milestone

Present executive campaign outcomes and ROI to an external board of marketing directors.

Term 4: 2-Month Paid Internship & Placements

Months 10–11
  • Guaranteed Paid Internship: 2 months full-time on-site/remote internship with established brands.
  • Leadership Placement Drives: Direct executive interviews for Manager and Specialist positions.
  • Portfolio & LinkedIn Optimization: Case study documentation, personal branding, salary negotiation.
  • 100% Placement Assurance: Dedicated placement manager until an offer letter is finalized.
Term 4 Milestone

Transition directly from paid internship into a full-time management or growth role (₹6.5L - ₹15L CTC).

Everything you need to know about the Master's in Digital Business PG Program.

It is an 11-month full-time postgraduate program designed for graduates and young professionals. It covers advanced performance marketing, data intelligence, marketing automation, e-commerce scaling, P&L management, and brand strategy, backed by a mandatory 2-month paid internship and 100% placement assurance.

Candidates must hold a Bachelor's degree (or be in their final semester of graduation) in any discipline—such as BBA, B.Com, BA, BCA, B.Sc, or B.Tech—with at least 50% aggregate marks. Working professionals with 0 to 3 years of work experience are also eligible.
